# Cauldron Rumble — Kongregate submission copy

## Short description

Fantasy autobattler with ingredient drafting, automatic merging, strategic
synergies and two handcrafted campaigns.

## Player-facing description

Build a magical cauldron and enter the grand tournament. Choose ingredients,
merge matching items into stronger versions, and create powerful combinations
around Fire, Poison, Guard, Frost and Echo. Battles resolve automatically, but
every purchase, reroll and family synergy shapes the result. Adapt to unusual
opponents, collect trophies and defeat the campaign bosses.

Cauldron Rumble supports English and German and saves campaign progress in the
player's browser.

AI disclosure: AI tools assisted with programming and the creation of some
visual assets, including backgrounds, characters, cauldrons, items and
promotional artwork. All such content was manually reviewed, edited and
integrated. No generative AI runs during gameplay.

## Instructions

- Mouse: click buttons, ingredients and item slots.
- Touch: tap buttons, ingredients and item slots.
- Buy five ingredients, merge matching items and build family synergies.
- Select **Start Battle** when the cauldron is ready.
- Battles play automatically. Claim the reward after each round and prepare for
  the next opponent.
